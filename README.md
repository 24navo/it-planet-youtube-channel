# IT-PLANET — YouTube Channel & Online Courses

Official GitHub repository for the **[IT-PLANET](https://www.youtube.com/@it-planet)** educational YouTube channel (`@it-planet`).

## About the Channel
**IT-PLANET** is an educational platform with **23,300+ subscribers** and **830+ video tutorials** dedicated to modern digital tools, artificial intelligence, and creative software.

### Main Topics & Playlists
- **AI & Neural Networks**: Practical guides on ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Midjourney, HeyGen, AI video editing, and smart automation.
- **Web Development from Scratch**: Step-by-step courses on responsive landing pages (`HTML + CSS + JS`), online stores (`WordPress + PHP + jQuery`), and web deployment.
- **Computer Graphics & Video Editing**: Comprehensive lessons on Adobe Photoshop, Premiere Pro, After Effects, and Adobe InDesign (layout of books, magazines, and e-books).
- **Tech & Automation**: Tutorials on Telegram AI bots, smart home integrations, and productivity tools.
